CHANGE
- Dashboard no longer shows a static-looking "Current trophy".
- It now shows the NEXT trophy you are working toward.
- Example: Bronze — 17 / 25 badges • 8 to unlock.
- After Bronze unlocks, it automatically switches to Silver progress.
- Then Gold, then Platinum.
- The progress bar is calculated against the current trophy target, so it visibly moves with every badge earned.
