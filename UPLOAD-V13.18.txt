ACTIVATE TRACKER v13.18 — PIN / UNPIN CLICK FIX

ROOT CAUSE
- Each badge card is clickable and carries data-open-focus-badge.
- The delegated click handler checked "open Focus" BEFORE checking the nested
  pin/unpin button.
- A tap on the pin button therefore matched the parent card first and opened
  Focus instead of changing the pin state.

FIX
- Pin, unpin, earned-state and explicit Open controls are now handled BEFORE
  the clickable badge-card action.
- Those nested controls stop propagation so the parent card cannot swallow them.
- Pin/unpin still saves immediately through the single saved pin-state function.
- Added type="button" / stronger touch handling to the generated pin controls.
