SMARTY'S ACTIVATE TRACKER v10.4.0 — MANUAL SPLASH

CHANGE
- Removed all automatic splash timers.
- Splash now stays on screen until you tap ENTER TRACKER.
- This avoids timing/service-worker issues completely.

SCALING
- Portrait/front splash uses splash-portrait.png and is treated as 9:16 artwork.
- Landscape/inner splash uses splash-landscape.png and is treated as 4:3 artwork.
- Images are never stretched; any unavoidable extra screen area is filled by the dark background.
- ENTER TRACKER respects Android safe-area spacing.

UPLOAD THESE FILES ONLY
- index.html
- app.js
- style.css
- sw.js

Upload to the ROOT of the GitHub repository and replace the existing files.
Do not replace your PNG artwork.
