SMARTY'S ACTIVATE TRACKER v8.8.0 — VENUE MAPPER UX UPDATE

NEW:
- Save & Stay
  Records the connection but keeps your current position unchanged.
  Ideal for side rooms / alcoves branching from the main corridor.

- Save & Move
  Records the connection and moves your current position to the next room.
  Ideal when continuing along the route.

EXAMPLE:
Current: Strike
Direction: Right
Next: Laser
Save & Stay

Then:
Current: Strike
Direction: Front
Next: Mega Grid
Save & Move

Replace same-named files, commit to main, wait 1–3 minutes, then refresh the web app.
