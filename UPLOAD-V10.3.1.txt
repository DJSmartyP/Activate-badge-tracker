SMARTY'S ACTIVATE TRACKER v10.3.1 — SPLASH RELEASE HOTFIX

BUG FIX
- v10.3 correctly enforced the 4-second splash hold but did not release the splash on a successful app load.
- This hotfix adds one controlled release function.
- Splash remains visible for a minimum of 4 seconds, then hides once the app is ready.
- Error states also release the splash after the same minimum hold.
